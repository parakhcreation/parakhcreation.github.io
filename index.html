About Parakh

Two doors, one
standard of quality.

Parakh — the word means the skill of judging something by sight and touch, of telling good cloth from ordinary the moment it's in your hands. It's the standard both our stores are built on.

Parakh Creation, on Hardutt Rai Chamaria Road, and Parakh Collection, on Dobson Road, are run with that same eye — sarees and suit sets selected piece by piece, not by the container-load. Between the two stores you'll find everyday cottons, festive silks, printed suit sets and pieces for special occasions, all chosen to be worn, not just displayed.

We believe the best way to choose a saree is still the old way: unfolded in daylight, held up against your shoulder, felt between your fingers. This website is here to help you shortlist before you come in — every final choice still happens at the counter.
